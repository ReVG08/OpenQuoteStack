# Code of conduct

Participation should be respectful, constructive and focused on the project.
Critique code and decisions without personal attacks. Harassment, threats,
discriminatory remarks, sexualized conduct, impersonation and publishing someone's
private information without consent are not acceptable.

Use good faith when interpreting questions and feedback. Keep technical disagreements
specific and evidence-based. Maintainers may remove inappropriate content or restrict
participation when conduct disrupts collaboration.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through their GitHub
profile contact options. Include relevant links and context; avoid publishing private
reports in issues. Reports will be handled with attention to privacy and conflicts
of interest. Do not assume an immediate response or a staffed moderation team.
